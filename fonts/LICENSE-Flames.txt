Flames
Designer: OutsideInside Fonts (Humberto Gillan)
Source: https://www.fontspace.com/flames-font-f25529

Digitization of the 1969 Mecanorma face “Dreamline.” The designer’s note on Fontspace:
“If you want to use it for a logo or something commercial, you don’t need to ask me, i do not charge nothing, you’re free to use it.”
Fontspace lists the face as freeware / 100% free.
